## Hi there 👋

Creating a final project based on CS50P:
https://cs50.harvard.edu/python/2022/project/

This project is about checking your bank account balance. 
In your first run, you will not have an account and so will be asked to create a new account by entering your name.
Once you have an account, you can deposit or withdraw your amounts. You can also view your past actions of deposits and withdrawals.
You can also delete your account should the service no longer interest you.
